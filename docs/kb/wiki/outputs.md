# Saved answers and wiki contributions

Updated 2026-11-06. Assistant-maintained review register. Human review is pending.

A saved report starts as needing review. After reading it, the librarian either files a supported durable insight into named topic pages or records why no change is needed. Filing is the assistant's evidence review, not human approval.

Each record includes the reviewed report hash, time, contribution, underlying evidence, and destination pages. A later edit to a report makes its current state need review again. Historical reports retain their original conclusions and dates.

The rows are derived from [memory.json](../memory.json). Supported findings must cite the underlying raw source or checked project evidence; an assistant output is not independent corroboration.
